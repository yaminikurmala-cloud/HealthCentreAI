# 🏥 Health Centre AI
### AI-Powered District Health Command Center

Health Centre AI is an intelligent healthcare management platform designed to digitally connect multiple Primary Health Centres (PHCs) under a single District Health Command Center. The platform provides real-time monitoring, AI-driven recommendations, predictive analytics, and smart resource allocation to help healthcare administrators make faster and data-driven decisions.

## 📌 Problem Statement

District healthcare administrators often manage multiple PHCs independently, making it difficult to monitor:

- Patient Footfall
- Medicine Availability
- Doctor Allocation
- Bed Occupancy
- Diagnostic Facilities
- Disease Trends

This results in delayed decisions, medicine shortages, poor resource utilization, and inefficient healthcare delivery.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Health Centre AI brings every PHC onto one centralized platform powered by Artificial Intelligence.

The system continuously analyzes live healthcare data and generates actionable recommendations to improve healthcare management across the district.
